Sleepwing vs the alternatives — an honest comparison

There are now many ways to keep an eye on your AI coding agents: official pets, open-source overlays, and classic break reminders. Most of them are good at what they do. Here is where Sleepwing actually differs — and when you should pick something else.

The one-sentence difference

Every alternative answers “what is my agent doing?”. Sleepwing answers “what should I do right now?” — it converts verified agent work time into small, bounded health breaks (water, stretch, look away), and calls you back the moment it is actually your turn.

When you should pick something else

Only use Codex and want a mascot? Codex Pets is built in — use it. Want a cross-platform pixel overlay with themes and no health opinion? clawd-on-desk is excellent. Just want eye-break reminders with no AI involved? Stretchly (free) or Lookaway (polished, paid) are the right tools. Sleepwing is for people who run multiple agents daily and keep sitting at the desk doing nothing while the machines work.
