宠物画廊Pet Gallery

做一只属于你的桌面伙伴 Make a companion that is yours

Sleepwing 内置两只官方角色，但最好的桌宠也许是你自己的：一个角色创意、一张角色图，甚至一张你家宠物的照片，都可以变成完整的动画桌宠。做好之后，欢迎投稿到社区画廊。 Sleepwing ships with two official characters, but the best companion might be your own: a character idea, character art, or even a photo of your real pet can become a fully animated companion. When it's done, share it with the community gallery.

怎么制作How to make one

制作在你自己的编程 Agent 里完成，Sleepwing 负责校验、导入和播放。全程本地，参考图与创意不会上传到任何服务器。 Production happens inside your own coding agent; Sleepwing validates, imports, and plays the result. Everything stays local — your art and ideas are never uploaded.

安装 perch-pet 技能Install the perch-pet skill

打开 Sleepwing → Pet Studio，一键安装 App 自带的 perch-pet Agent 技能。只有用户环境中已安装 hatch-pet 时才会复用它。Open Sleepwing → Pet Studio and install the app-bundled perch-pet agent skill with one click. It reuses hatch-pet only when that optional skill is already installed.

描述角色或给参考图Describe or show your character

在 Agent 里描述外形、材质和性格，或提供参考图。Pet Studio 的「从照片开始」还能在本机把照片主体抠成干净的透明参考图。技能会产出完整的 8×11 动画图集。Describe the look, material, and personality in your agent, or supply reference art. Pet Studio's "Start from a photo" can lift the subject from a photo into clean transparent reference art, fully on device. The skill produces a complete 8×11 animation atlas.

导入并预览Import and preview

回到 Pet Studio 导入生成的 .perchpet。Sleepwing 会校验结构与素材、拒绝符号链接，然后用真实的待机动画实时预览。Import the generated .perchpet back in Pet Studio. Sleepwing validates structure and assets, rejects symbolic links, then previews the real idle motion live.

完整教程见首页的「导入自定义桌宠」一节。 See "Import a custom pet" on the home page for the full walkthrough.

投稿一只桌宠Submit a pet

投稿方式与规则How to submit, and the rules

  • 在 GitHub 提交一个标题以 [Pet showcase] 开头的 Issue，附上：角色名与一句话介绍、一张预览图（待机帧或动图）、可下载的 .perchpet 链接。Open a GitHub issue titled [Pet showcase] with: the character's name and a one-line intro, a preview image (idle frame or animation), and a downloadable .perchpet link.
  • 必须是原创角色，或你拥有使用权的形象；不接受受版权保护的第三方角色。Original characters only, or art you have the rights to; no copyrighted third-party characters.
  • 内容需适合所有年龄。Content must be appropriate for all ages.
  • 你的作品版权归你：用户自制宠物属于创作者本人（见 素材许可），入选画廊仅表示展示与链接。Your work stays yours: user-made pets belong to their creators (see the assets license). Being featured only means we display and link to it.

Sleepwing 应用本体不联网：画廊只存在于这个网站，下载的素材包始终由你在 Pet Studio 中手动导入并经过完整校验。 The Sleepwing app itself makes no network connections: the gallery lives only on this site, and downloaded packages are always imported manually through Pet Studio with full validation.
